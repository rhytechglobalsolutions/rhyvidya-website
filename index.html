The Hindi class ladder.

RhyVidya's stages follow the recognised Hindi class path — from your very first words all the way to degree-level proficiency. Saraswathi places every student on the right rung and prepares you for the next.

  1. 1

    Parichaya Introductory

    The absolute foundational step — where every Hindi journey begins.

  2. 2

    Prathamic Primary

    Your first major structured exam, evaluating elementary reading and writing.

  3. 3

    Madhyama Intermediate

    The “Mathima” level — it tests intermediate grammar, short stories, and composition.

  4. 4

    Rashtrabhasha Advanced

    Translates roughly to high-school level proficiency in Hindi.

  5. 5

    Praveshika Higher

    Marks the transition into higher Hindi literature.

  6. 6

    Visharad & Praveen Higher levels

    The highest rungs — Visharad and Praveen sequentially align with university BA and MA degrees in Hindi language proficiency.

Good to know.

Who can learn with RhyVidya?

RhyVidya is for students worldwide who want to learn live with experienced teachers. We are starting with Hindi; Tamil is next, followed by Physics and Mathematics. The final supported age range will be confirmed before registration opens.

Will RhyVidya be easy to use?

Yes. The product is planned around a straightforward path: register, find the right Hindi stage, book a time, and join class in one tap. Large controls, plain language, mobile-friendly pages, and local-time appointments keep the experience clear.

Which platform are classes held on?

Classes are held online, on whichever platform suits you best — Google Meet, Zoho Meeting, YouTube, or WhatsApp. Each supports live video, student interaction, screen sharing, chat, and board teaching, and WhatsApp stays at hand for reminders, quick questions, and backup communication.

What is included in the launch offer?

The first month is free when RhyVidya launches. If a student continues afterward, the paid plan begins with one month’s fee as a deposit plus the selected subscription payment.

Which payment plans will be available?

Families can choose monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or yearly billing. Longer subscriptions will receive larger discounts; exact tuition and discount percentages will be published before enrollment opens.

Can I choose 1-to-1 or group classes?

Yes. RhyVidya offers 1-to-1 classes and group sessions, on weekdays and weekends — all by appointment. Pick your format, date, and time on the booking calendar, and your teacher confirms the appointment and online class link by email.

Can I watch RhyVidya videos on YouTube?

Yes. RhyVidya's practice videos are published on the Rhytech Global Solutions YouTube channel, and Phase 1 videos stay free to watch there.

Which subjects are coming next?

Tamil is next, followed by Physics and Mathematics — each taught live by an experienced teacher, like every subject on RhyVidya. Hindi is our launch subject and is live now. You can register your interest for the upcoming subjects in the booking form today.
